# Verity Study

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students studying from uploaded course material and following up on AI explanations.

## Product Purpose

A conversation-first study workspace that makes explanations understandable and
their relationship to source material inspectable.

## Operating Context

Local, single-user frontend connected to a FastAPI backend. Conversations and
answer metadata are persisted by the backend. Existing React, TypeScript,
Tailwind, vinext, and lucide components remain the implementation stack.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Text and screenshot questions, follow-up context, persistent conversation search,
  rename, deletion, and Markdown export.
- Auto, My sources, and General knowledge answer scopes. Document citations,
  evidence inspection, token grounding, conflict notices, and image analysis.
- Document and lecture ingestion, library search and removal; summaries,
  comparisons, and practice questions.
- Redesign preserves backend contracts, existing content, and working behavior.
- Desktop and mobile web; keyboard operation and reduced-motion support.

## Brand Commitments

Verity Study. Dark-only design, with no theme switching or glassmorphism.
Apple-quality clarity and precision are a craft reference, not a copied identity.
Conversation-first navigation was explicitly confirmed by the user.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing source files and saved conversations available through the running local
backend. No fabricated usage statistics, testimonials, or generated study content
may be displayed as real user material.

## Product Principles

- Reading and asking outrank diagnostic metadata.
- Source-supported and general-knowledge content remain distinguishable.
- Secondary tools are available without competing with the conversation.
- A visual redesign must not discard saved history or change answer semantics.
